Privacy Policy

LunaTime is a sleep calculator and relaxation app by Potato Apps.

LunaTime is a sleep calculator and relaxation app by Potato Apps.

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy applies to the LunaTime Android app made by Potato Apps.

LunaTime helps users plan sleep and wake-up times based on sleep cycles. The app also includes related sleep and relaxation features such as bedtime reminders, alarm actions, Relax Mode, breathing exercises, sleep sounds, haptics, widgets, and theme customization.

This policy explains how LunaTime handles user data.

Information We Collect

The current version of LunaTime does not require users to create an account or sign in.

LunaTime is not designed to collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

LunaTime does not ask for:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Health records
  • Payment card details

App Data and Preferences

LunaTime may let users enter or select sleep-related information, such as bedtime, wake-up time, reminder choices, alarm actions, sleep sound preferences, breathing exercise settings, haptic settings, widget preferences, and theme settings.

These features are intended to work on the user's own device.

Potato Apps does not upload this information to its own servers.

Sleep Calculator and Relax Features

Sleep cycle calculations, bedtime suggestions, wake-up suggestions, breathing exercises, sleep sound selections, haptic settings, and similar app features are handled locally on the user's device.

LunaTime does not need names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, payment information, or location data for its main sleep calculator and relaxation features.

Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

The current version of LunaTime does not contain ads.

LunaTime does not intentionally use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Tracking pixels
  • Analytics tools
  • Third-party tracking SDKs
  • User profiling tools

The current version of LunaTime does not require user accounts or subscriptions for its main sleep calculator features.

Optional Paid Features and Future Updates

LunaTime may add optional paid features in the future, such as premium Relax tools, breathing exercises, sleep sounds, customization options, monthly subscriptions, or other premium features.

Paid features have not been added to the current version unless clearly shown inside the app.

If optional paid digital features or subscriptions are added in the future, purchases may be processed through Google Play Billing or another payment method allowed by Google Play policies. Potato Apps does not receive full payment card details from users.

If future updates add online services, analytics, subscriptions, third-party SDKs, cloud sync, accounts, or features that collect data, this privacy policy will be updated before or when those features are released.

Any paid feature will be clearly shown before purchase.

Data Sharing

Potato Apps does not sell, rent, trade, or share LunaTime user data with third parties.

If LunaTime features change in the future in a way that affects data handling, this policy will be updated.

Health Disclaimer

LunaTime provides general sleep timing and relaxation suggestions. It is not a medical app or medical device.

LunaTime does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention for any medical condition.

If users have serious, ongoing, or concerning sleep problems, they should speak with a qualified healthcare professional.

Children’s Privacy

LunaTime is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Potato Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children through LunaTime.

External Links

LunaTime may include links to external websites, such as Potato Apps support pages, privacy pages, or Google Play pages.

When users open an external link, they leave the LunaTime app. External websites have their own privacy policies and practices. Potato Apps is not responsible for how external websites collect or handle information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if LunaTime features change.

The date at the top of this page will show the most recent update.
