LunaTime Privacy Policy

LunaTime is developed by Potato Apps.

Effective date: July 15, 2026

Your privacy

LunaTime is designed to work without collecting your personal information.

You do not need an account to use LunaTime. Your sleep times, preferences, reminders, breathing settings, sound choices, and saved sound mixes are stored on your device. Potato Apps does not receive or have access to this information.

LunaTime does not use advertising, in-app analytics, or tracking, and Potato Apps does not sell your information.

Information stored on your device

LunaTime stores the information needed for its features, such as:

  • sleep and wake-up times
  • fall-asleep time and cycle-length settings
  • selected sleep profile
  • bedtime reminder settings
  • theme and time-format preferences
  • breathing-session settings
  • haptic and sound preferences
  • saved sound mixes

This information stays inside LunaTime on your device.

LunaTime does not currently provide its own account system, cloud synchronization, or online backup service.

Notifications and alarms

LunaTime may ask for notification permission when you choose to create a bedtime reminder.

Reminder information is handled on your device. Potato Apps does not receive your reminder times or notification history.

When you choose to set an alarm, LunaTime may open your device’s clock or alarm app with a suggested time. LunaTime does not read your existing alarms.

Sounds and haptics

LunaTime can play relaxation sounds and use your device’s vibration motor during breathing sessions.

These features do not record audio and do not use your microphone.

Google Play and technical diagnostics

LunaTime does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting service.

Google Play and Android may provide Potato Apps with aggregated information such as installs, uninstalls, device types, Android versions, crashes, and app-not-responding reports. This information is provided through Google Play and is used to understand technical problems and improve the app.

It does not include the sleep times, reminders, breathing sessions, sound mixes, or other information you enter inside LunaTime.

Google Play provides developers with app statistics and Android Vitals reports for technical quality and stability. Google Support

Device backups

Depending on your Android and device backup settings, Android may include some LunaTime app data in a system backup or device-to-device transfer.

These backups are controlled by Android, Google, or your device manufacturer. Potato Apps does not receive or have access to them.

Android supports automatic app-data backups and device-to-device restoration on supported devices. Android Developers

Data deletion

You can delete LunaTime’s local data by:

  • clearing LunaTime’s storage from your Android device settings, or
  • uninstalling LunaTime

Uninstalling normally removes information stored locally by the app. Information included in an Android backup may remain until it is removed through your device or Google backup settings.

Potato Apps cannot view or delete Android backups on your behalf.

Support messages

If you contact Potato Apps by email, Potato Apps will receive the email address, message, and any files or technical details you choose to send.

This information is used only to respond to your message, investigate problems, and keep necessary support records. You can request deletion of a support message where applicable.

Children’s privacy

LunaTime does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

The sleep-profile options in LunaTime provide general age-based guidance. Your selected profile stays on your device and is not sent to Potato Apps.

Sleep guidance

LunaTime provides general sleep estimates and relaxation tools. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if LunaTime’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.
