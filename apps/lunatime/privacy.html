LunaTime Privacy Policy

LunaTime is developed by Potato Apps, the developer listed on Google Play as PotatoApps LK.

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information LunaTime uses, what stays on your device, and how purchases and support requests are handled.

You do not need a LunaTime account. Your sleep records and personal settings stay on your device during normal use. Google Play and RevenueCat process information needed for purchases, Plus access, and related technical services.

LunaTime does not display advertisements. Potato Apps does not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

LunaTime stores information needed for its features, including:

  • Your first name and selected sleep profile.
  • Sleep and wake times, calculator settings, plans, and reminders.
  • Sleep check-ins, moods, sleep quality, and notes.
  • Breathing settings, sound choices, favourites, and saved mixes.
  • Appearance preferences and free Plus access status.

This information is not automatically sent to Potato Apps or RevenueCat. We cannot view it unless you choose to share it with us, such as in a support message.

LunaTime does not provide its own cloud account or cloud synchronization service.

Purchases and LunaTime Plus

Google Play processes payments. LunaTime does not receive your full payment-card details.

We use RevenueCat to verify purchases, maintain Plus access, and restore purchases. Information processed for these purposes includes:

  • A generated customer identifier.
  • Purchase references and purchased products.
  • Subscription status, renewal and expiration information, and refunds.
  • Prices and currencies.
  • Technical information such as device model, Android version, app version, and language settings.

The generated identifier connects the app with its purchase-related records. It does not require a LunaTime login, but it can be associated with purchases.

RevenueCat can receive information when LunaTime opens or resumes, even before you make a purchase. LunaTime does not send your first name, sleep records, notes, or sound preferences to RevenueCat.

Potato Apps can view purchase and subscription reporting through RevenueCat. These records can include the last connection time and a country obtained from transaction information or inferred from an IP address. RevenueCat states that it discards the IP address after determining the country. LunaTime does not request GPS location access.

We have not enabled additional RevenueCat integrations that forward customer events to external analytics or marketing platforms.

Further information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat privacy information

Technical information and diagnostics

Google Play’s billing services process technical information about payment operations, including errors and connection results, to improve reliability and troubleshoot problems.

Where Google Play services are available, LunaTime uses them to check for updates and offer an in-app review option. A review is submitted only when you choose to submit it.

Google Play may provide Potato Apps with installation statistics, device and Android-version information, and crash or app-not-responding reports.

LunaTime does not include a separate advertising or general behavioural analytics service. Purchase reporting and technical diagnostics do not include your local sleep records or notes.

Notifications, alarms, sounds, and haptics

LunaTime may request notification permission for reminders. Reminder schedules are handled on your device and are not sent to Potato Apps.

Background audio uses Android’s media playback services. When you choose to set an alarm, LunaTime may open your device’s clock app with a suggested time. LunaTime does not read your existing alarms.

Relaxation sounds and breathing haptics do not record audio or use your microphone. The current sound files and artwork are included with the app.

Backups

You can manually export supported settings, sleep check-ins, notes, plans, favourites, and saved sound mixes.

LunaTime does not encrypt exported backup files. Store them somewhere you trust.

You choose where to save the file. If you select a cloud storage service, that provider may upload and store it under its own privacy policy. Potato Apps does not operate that service.

Exported files remain outside LunaTime’s private storage and must be deleted separately.

LunaTime is configured to exclude its main sleep database and settings files from standard Android automatic app backups. Separately, Google services may preserve a purchase-related identifier used by RevenueCat to help restore access after reinstalling or changing devices. This depends on your device and backup settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send.

LunaTime’s feedback option may prepare an editable email containing app and device information. You can review or remove that information before sending.

We use these messages to respond to your request, investigate problems, and handle related follow-up. Please do not send payment-card details.

Why we process information

We process purchase-related information to provide and maintain the Plus features you purchase.

We use necessary technical information and support correspondence to operate the app, resolve problems, and respond to requests.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract for processing necessary to provide purchased services.
  • Legitimate interests in maintaining reliable services, understanding purchase performance, and responding to support requests, while considering your privacy rights.
  • Legal obligations where records must be retained or disclosed by law.
  • Consent where required for a particular activity.

An Android permission is not, by itself, consent to unrelated uses of your information.

Service providers and international processing

Google Play and RevenueCat process the information described in this policy. RevenueCat acts as our service provider for purchase management; Google also processes information under its own terms.

These providers may process information outside Sri Lanka or your country of residence, including in the United States.

Where required, international transfers must use applicable safeguards. RevenueCat’s Data Processing Addendum describes its contractual safeguards, including provisions for transfers of European and UK personal data.

Retention and deletion

Information stored within LunaTime remains on your device until you remove it using an available app control, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.

Exported backups remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Purchase and service records may remain with Google Play and RevenueCat after uninstalling. Retention depends on the purposes of those records and applicable service, legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution requirements.

We retain support messages for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal requirements. You can contact us to request removal of correspondence you sent.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not automatically delete exported files, support emails, Google Play records, RevenueCat records, or purchase-related identifiers preserved by Google services.

For requests concerning information held by Potato Apps or processed on our behalf, email potatoappsdev@gmail.com. We may need information to locate the record and verify the request. An email address alone may not identify a RevenueCat record because LunaTime does not associate your email with its generated customer identifier.

Some information may need to be retained where permitted or required by law. Requests concerning information Google holds independently are subject to Google’s procedures.

If you use LunaTime again after a RevenueCat record is deleted, the app may create a record again to provide purchase services and check Plus access.

Uninstalling LunaTime or deleting purchase-related records does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact us to make a request. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, including the authority in your European country where applicable.

Security

Connections used by LunaTime’s RevenueCat and Google Play services encrypt information in transit.

App-local information uses Android’s app storage protections. Exported backup files are outside those protections and are not encrypted by LunaTime.

No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Children’s privacy

LunaTime is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact potatoappsdev@gmail.com so we can investigate and address it.

External links

LunaTime may open websites, Google Play, an email app, or a storage service you select. Those services’ privacy policies apply to information they handle independently.

This policy covers the LunaTime Android app. Website privacy practices may be described separately.

Sleep guidance

LunaTime provides general sleep estimates and relaxation tools. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when LunaTime’s features or information-handling practices change. The effective date identifies the latest revision.
