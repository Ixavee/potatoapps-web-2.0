Krate Privacy Policy

Krate is developed by Potato Apps.

Effective date: 7/15/2026

Introduction

Krate is developed by Potato Apps.

The short version

Krate is designed to keep your grocery information private.

  • You do not need an account.
  • Your grocery lists, prices, budgets, reminders, and shopping history stay on your device.
  • Potato Apps cannot see this information.
  • Krate does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • Potato Apps does not sell your information.

Information stored on your device

Krate stores the information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Grocery items, quantities, categories, and prices
  • Items you need, have collected, or are running low on
  • Shopping trips and monthly budgets
  • Reminders you choose to create
  • Barcodes and product details you save
  • Your currency, theme, and other app settings

This information is stored inside Krate on your device. Potato Apps does not receive it or keep a copy on its own servers.

Krate does not currently provide its own cloud backup or synchronization. Android may back up eligible app data depending on your device and backup settings.

Barcode scanning

When you choose “Scan barcode,” Krate uses your camera to read the barcode on your device.

Krate does not save or upload photos or videos from your camera. The barcode is read directly on your phone.

Barcode scanning uses technology provided by Google. Google may receive limited technical information, such as your device type, app version, and performance or error information, to maintain that technology. Your camera image and barcode contents are not sent to Google for barcode recognition.

You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Reminders and notifications

Krate only asks for notification permission when you choose to use reminders.

Your reminder details remain on your device. Krate does not use notifications for advertising or unrelated messages.

Sending feedback

The “Send feedback” option opens your email app with a message addressed to Potato Apps.

The message may include basic technical details such as:

  • Your Krate version
  • Your Android version
  • Your device manufacturer and model

It does not automatically include your grocery lists, prices, budgets, reminders, barcodes, or shopping history.

Nothing is sent unless you review the email and choose to send it. If you send feedback, Potato Apps will receive the email address and information included in your message and may retain it as needed to respond or resolve the reported issue.

Sharing and selling information

Potato Apps does not sell your information.

Krate does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Information is only transmitted in the limited situations explained above, such as technical diagnostics from the barcode-scanning technology or an email you choose to send.

Your choices and deleting your information

You can edit or delete your grocery information inside Krate.

You can also remove Krate’s local information by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app. Android backups may remain or restore information according to your device’s backup settings.

Because Potato Apps does not receive your grocery information, you do not need to contact us to delete it from our servers.

If you previously sent a feedback email, you may contact us to request the deletion of that correspondence.

Children’s privacy

Krate is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. Potato Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Krate.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Krate’s features change.

If future versions introduce online services, subscriptions, cloud features, or optional AI features, this policy will be updated before those features are released. The effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.
