Krate

Offline-first grocery manager by Potato Apps

IN DEVELOPMENT

Plan groceries, track spending, and shop with a clearer budget.

Krate is an offline-first grocery manager for shopping lists, prices, reminders, budgets, and shopping history. It is being built to make everyday grocery planning clear and easy to manage.

App Support Privacy Policy
WHAT IT DOES

A clearer way to plan groceries and understand spending.

✓

Build shopping lists with quantities, units, categories, and optional prices

✓

Organize items as Need, Got it, or Running low

✓

See estimated totals and identify items with missing prices

✓

Create reminders for items that are running low

✓

Finish shopping trips and keep a history of purchased items

✓

Set a monthly grocery budget and review spending across trips

✓

Scan and save product barcodes

✓

Keep grocery information locally on the device without creating an account

Plan the list

Quickly add grocery items, quantities, units, categories, and optional prices before shopping.

Track the trip

Mark items as collected, follow your progress, and save completed trips with their purchased items.

Understand spending

Set a monthly budget and review grocery spending across completed shopping trips.
