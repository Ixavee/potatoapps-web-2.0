Potato Apps Website Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Potato Apps website handles privacy.

Effective date: July 15, 2026

It applies to the Potato Apps website only. Each Potato Apps application may have its own privacy policy explaining how that app handles information and permissions.

Information you provide

You do not need an account to use the Potato Apps website.

The website does not include registration, login, payment, or profile features, and Potato Apps does not directly ask visitors to provide personal information through the website.

If you choose to contact Potato Apps by email, Potato Apps will receive your email address, message, and any files or information you choose to send. This information is used to read and respond to your message and maintain necessary support records.

Website analytics and advertising

Potato Apps does not use:

  • advertising trackers
  • behavioural advertising
  • analytics tools
  • tracking pixels
  • visitor profiles
  • marketing cookies

Potato Apps does not sell personal information.

Website hosting

The Potato Apps website is hosted by Vercel.

When you visit the website, Vercel may automatically process limited technical information required to deliver, protect, and operate the site. This may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • browser and device type
  • operating system
  • approximate location based on IP address
  • request, performance, diagnostic, and security logs

This information is processed by Vercel under its own privacy terms. Potato Apps does not use this information to build advertising profiles or track visitors across other websites.

Vercel states that its services may process request logs, IP addresses, device information, diagnostics, and approximate location derived from an IP address. Vercel Privacy Notice

External links

The website may link to external services such as Google Play, GitHub, Ko-fi, social platforms, or other websites.

When you open an external link, you leave the Potato Apps website. Those services have their own privacy policies and data practices, and Potato Apps does not control how they handle information.

App privacy policies

Potato Apps applications may store or process different types of information depending on their features.

The website provides separate privacy policies for supported apps. The privacy policy for an individual app applies when you use that app.

Data sharing

Potato Apps does not sell, rent, or trade personal information collected through the website.

Information may be processed by service providers only when needed to host, secure, or operate the website, or when required by law.

Children’s privacy

The Potato Apps website is not designed to collect personal information from children, and Potato Apps does not knowingly request personal information from children through the website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website, its hosting, or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.
